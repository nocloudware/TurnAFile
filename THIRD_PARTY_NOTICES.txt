PDF FORMATTING PRESERVATION
---------------------------
TurnAFile preserves PDF/document formatting through a combination of:
  - Open XML SDK (MIT) for reading DOCX structure (styles, colors, fonts, tables)
  - Custom HTML renderer for HTML→PDF rendering
  - QuestPDF (MIT) for final PDF document assembly
  - Custom lightweight HTML parser (in-house, MIT-compatible) for HTML→PDF text extraction
